# The Ghana Growers Blueprint

Version 1.0

This blueprint defines the identity, design language, and product strategy for Ghana Growers. It should guide product decisions, public copy, page structure, visual design, and future feature planning.

## Chapter 1: Identity

### Why Ghana Growers Exists

Ghana Growers exists because farmers deserve better access to information, markets, trusted buyers, reliable suppliers, and practical tools that help them farm and sell with confidence.

Many farmers work hard but still struggle with the same problems:

- Limited access to serious buyers
- Unstable market prices
- Unreliable logistics
- Poor visibility
- Low trust between farmers and buyers
- Lack of practical digital tools
- Limited access to quality inputs and services

Ghana Growers was created to close that gap.

We are building a platform that helps farmers, buyers, and suppliers connect in a more organized, trusted, and practical way.

Ghana Growers is not just a marketplace. It is a growing agricultural ecosystem for Ghana.

### Our Vision

To become Ghana's most trusted agricultural platform, helping farmers access markets, information, tools, suppliers, and opportunities in one place.

Long term, Ghana Growers should become the platform people think of when they need to:

- Find farmers
- Buy farm produce
- Sell farm produce
- Check market prices
- Access farming tools
- Find agricultural suppliers
- Connect with reliable partners
- Build stronger agricultural value chains

### Our Mission

Our mission is to make agriculture easier, more transparent, and more rewarding for Ghanaian farmers, buyers, and suppliers.

We do this by building tools and systems that improve:

- Market access
- Trust
- Produce quality
- Communication
- Visibility
- Sourcing
- Farmer support
- Supplier access
- Agricultural decision-making

### The Ghana Growers Promise

We promise to build a platform that:

- Puts Ghanaian farmers first
- Creates real market opportunities
- Helps buyers source produce more easily
- Supports trusted suppliers
- Promotes quality farm produce
- Encourages reliable logistics
- Supports sustainable farming practices
- Rewards professionalism and trust
- Makes agriculture easier to navigate

Every feature we build should support at least one of these promises. If it does not, we should not build it.

### Who We Build For

#### Primary Audience: Farmers

Farmers are at the center of Ghana Growers.

We build for farmers who want to:

- Sell produce
- Find buyers
- Improve visibility
- Access market prices
- Use farming tools
- Connect with suppliers
- Become more professional
- Grow their farming business

Every major decision should ask: Will this help a Ghanaian farmer?

#### Secondary Audience: Buyers

Buyers are the demand side of Ghana Growers.

We build for buyers who want to:

- Source farm produce
- Find reliable farmers
- Submit buyer requests
- Compare available supply
- Reduce sourcing stress
- Build trusted supply relationships

Buyers should feel that Ghana Growers helps them source produce, not just browse listings.

#### Third Audience: Suppliers

Suppliers help farmers succeed.

We build for suppliers who provide:

- Seeds
- Fertilizer
- Irrigation
- Tools
- Packaging
- Logistics
- Mechanization
- Advisory
- Storage
- Farm services

Suppliers should see Ghana Growers as a trusted channel to reach farmers and agricultural buyers.

### What Ghana Growers Is

Ghana Growers is:

- A farmer network
- A marketplace
- A supplier directory
- A buyer request system
- A farmer support platform
- A digital farming tool hub
- A trust and quality framework
- An agricultural ecosystem

Ghana Growers is not only a website. It is a system that supports agriculture from information to market access.

### What Ghana Growers Is Not

Ghana Growers is not:

- A charity
- A random WhatsApp group
- A generic marketplace
- A directory with no quality control
- A platform full of fake profiles
- A place for unverified claims
- A system that leaves buyers and farmers alone after connection

Ghana Growers should feel organized, trustworthy, and intentional.

### The Ghana Growers Way

The Ghana Growers Way is simple:

- Help first
- Build trust
- Create opportunity
- Keep things practical

Farmers should not need complicated software to benefit from Ghana Growers.

Buyers should not need to call ten people to find produce.

Suppliers should not struggle to reach serious farmers.

The platform should reduce confusion, not add to it.

### Core Product Belief

Daily value comes before transactions.

If farmers only visit Ghana Growers when they want to sell, usage will be occasional.

If farmers visit Ghana Growers to check weather, market prices, crop health, and farming advice, Ghana Growers becomes part of their daily farming life.

That is why Farmer Hub is central to the platform.

Farmer Hub creates daily usefulness.

The marketplace creates business opportunities.

The directory builds trust.

The quality standard strengthens confidence.

Together, they create the Ghana Growers ecosystem.

### Core Principles

1. Farmers first
2. Trust before transactions
3. Daily value
4. Simplicity wins
5. One purpose per screen
6. Guide the user
7. Premium means calm
8. Real over perfect
9. Ghana first
10. Build an ecosystem

### The Three Decision Filters

Every new feature, page, or campaign must improve at least one of these:

- Trust
- Daily value
- Revenue

If it does not improve one of these, it should wait.

### Long-Term Vision

Ghana Growers should become the operating system for agriculture in Ghana.

When someone needs to know today's market prices, they check Ghana Growers.

When a farmer needs buyers, they use Ghana Growers.

When a buyer needs produce, they submit a request through Ghana Growers.

When a supplier wants to reach farmers, they join Ghana Growers.

When a crop has a problem, farmers use Farmer Hub.

When quality matters, buyers look for Ghana Growers Quality Standard members.

That is the future we are building toward.

### Chapter Summary

Ghana Growers exists to make agriculture in Ghana more trusted, connected, and opportunity-driven.

We are not building just a website.

We are building a platform that supports farmers, buyers, suppliers, and the wider agricultural value chain.

The foundation is simple:

- Farmers first
- Trust before transactions
- Daily value before occasional use
- Simplicity over complexity
- Ghana first
- Ecosystem over isolated features

## Chapter 2: Brand Identity and Design Language

### Brand Philosophy

Ghana Growers is more than a website.

It is a trusted agricultural platform built to connect farmers, buyers, suppliers, and opportunities across Ghana.

Every visual decision should reinforce one feeling: trust.

We do not compete by looking louder.

We compete by looking calmer, simpler, and more intentional.

Every page should feel professional, human, and easy to understand.

If a design creates confusion, we redesign it.

If a design adds unnecessary complexity, we simplify it.

Our visual identity should always reflect the values of Ghana Growers:

- Trust
- Opportunity
- Quality
- Growth
- Simplicity

### Brand Personality

If Ghana Growers were a person, it would be:

- Professional
- Reliable
- Knowledgeable
- Warm
- Calm
- Optimistic
- Practical
- Approachable

Never:

- Flashy
- Corporate
- Complicated
- Aggressive
- Overdesigned

Technology should feel invisible. The focus should always remain on people and agriculture.

### The Feeling We Want

When someone visits Ghana Growers they should immediately feel:

- I can trust this platform.
- This company understands agriculture.
- I know where to begin.
- I want to explore.
- I will come back.

Every page should reduce uncertainty. Every page should create confidence.

### Color Philosophy

Our colors are inspired by nature, agriculture, harvest, and trust.

Not technology. Not finance. Not corporate software.

The palette should feel timeless.

| Color | Hex | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest Trust | `#143A1F` | Primary buttons, navigation, footer, trust elements, primary icons, logo text |
| Leaf Green | `#4E7A3D` | Secondary accents, soft highlights, hover states, illustrations, natural emphasis |
| Morning Ivory | `#F7F6EF` | Hero backgrounds, warm page backgrounds, editorial sections |
| Light Sage | `#EEF3E8` | Farmer Hub, information panels, soft feature areas, Quality Standard |
| Harvest Gold | `#E1B75A` | Labels, highlights, small icons, status indicators, hover accents |
| Cocoa Earth | `#6B4A2F` | Agricultural warmth, illustrations, charts, small accents |
| Forest Ink | `#162016` | Headings and primary content |
| Sage Gray | `rgba(22,32,22,0.68)` | Body copy and supporting information |
| Border Mist | `rgba(20,58,31,0.10)` | Cards, forms, dividers |

Harvest Gold should attract attention, not dominate the page.

Cocoa Earth should never be used for large backgrounds.

Never use pure black for primary text.

### Typography

Typography should feel editorial, professional, calm, readable, and confident.

Primary typeface: Inter.

Use Inter for:

- Navigation
- Body text
- Forms
- Cards
- Buttons
- Tables
- Dashboard
- Marketplace
- Farmer Hub tools
- Admin

Editorial typeface: Cormorant Garamond.

Use Cormorant Garamond only for:

- Homepage hero
- Major marketing page heroes
- About page
- GG Quality Standard page
- Major campaign headlines

Never use serif for UI components.

Typography rules:

- Headings are short.
- Paragraphs are short.
- Never shout.
- If everything is bold, nothing is important.
- Whitespace is part of typography.

### Photography

Photography is one of Ghana Growers' strongest trust signals.

Every image should feel like it belongs to the same story.

Preferred style:

- Real Ghanaian agriculture
- Natural light
- Authentic people
- Real farms
- Real produce
- Human interaction
- Documentary style
- Warm earth tones
- Consistent lighting

Avoid:

- Obvious AI imagery
- Cold stock photos
- Over-edited images
- Mixed photography styles
- Artificial smiles

Every homepage image should look as if it came from the same photographer.

### Icon Language

Icons should support content. They should never dominate it.

Use subtle colored backgrounds:

- Marketplace: Forest Trust
- Farmer Hub: Light Sage
- Quality Standard: Morning Ivory
- Warnings: Harvest Gold
- Success: Soft Green

Avoid using the same icon treatment everywhere.

### Buttons

Buttons guide action. They do not decorate pages.

Rules:

- One primary button per section.
- Secondary actions use outline buttons.
- Avoid multiple competing primary actions.
- Cards should often be fully clickable instead of containing unnecessary buttons.

### Cards

Cards exist to communicate one idea.

One card. One purpose. One action.

Cards should:

- Feel light
- Have generous padding
- Contain minimal text
- Use soft shadows
- Never become miniature webpages

### Whitespace

Whitespace is not empty space.

Whitespace creates:

- Luxury
- Confidence
- Focus
- Breathing room

Never fill space simply because it exists.

### Motion

Motion should be subtle.

- Fade transitions: about 200ms
- Card lift: about 4px
- Image zoom: about 1.03x
- Buttons: soft color change only

Avoid bouncing, spinning, and heavy animations.

Motion should support confidence, not attract attention.

### Homepage Philosophy

Every homepage section must communicate one idea.

Never combine multiple ideas.

The homepage should tell this story:

1. Welcome
2. Marketplace
3. Farmer Hub
4. Quality Standard
5. Featured Farmers
6. Join the Network

Nothing unnecessary.

### Copywriting

Write for people, not software.

Prefer:

- Find Buyers
- Sell Produce
- Check Prices
- Upload Crop Photo
- Need Produce?

Avoid:

- Buzzwords
- Corporate language
- Long explanations
- Complex sentences

Use simple words, short paragraphs, and active voice.

### Mobile First

Most Ghana Growers users will visit on mobile.

Always design for mobile first.

Rules:

- Thumb-friendly
- Simple forms
- Large tap areas
- No horizontal scrolling
- No hidden navigation
- Fast understanding

### Premium Means Calm

Premium does not mean more gradients, more cards, more shadows, or more animations.

Premium means restraint, consistency, confidence, high-quality imagery, excellent spacing, and simple navigation.

### What We Never Do

We never:

- Use unnecessary gradients
- Create dashboard-looking public pages
- Repeat information
- Use decorative elements without purpose
- Overuse buttons
- Overload pages with text
- Make users search for important actions

### The Ghana Growers Design Test

Before approving any page ask:

- Does this feel calm?
- Is the main action obvious?
- Would a Ghanaian farmer understand this in 10 seconds?
- Does this build trust?
- Have we removed everything unnecessary?

If any answer is no, the design is not finished.

### Final Principle

We are not trying to become the most beautiful agricultural website.

We are building the most trusted agricultural platform in Ghana.

Every color, photograph, button, sentence, and interaction should strengthen that trust.

When in doubt: remove, simplify, refine.

## Chapter 3: Product Strategy and Ecosystem

### Product Philosophy

Ghana Growers is not a collection of features.

It is a connected agricultural ecosystem.

Every product, page, and feature should strengthen the others.

Nothing should exist in isolation.

Every new feature must answer one question: Which part of the ecosystem does this strengthen?

If the answer is none, we do not build it.

### The Ghana Growers Ecosystem

The platform is built around five pillars.

#### Pillar 1: Daily Value

Farmer Hub is the heartbeat of Ghana Growers.

Farmer Hub exists to help farmers make better decisions every day.

It creates habit, builds trust, and keeps farmers returning.

Farmer Hub is not a marketplace. It is a decision-support platform.

Core tools:

- Crop Health
- Live Weather
- Farm Assistant
- Current Market Prices
- Daily Insights, future
- Regional Alerts, future

Success is measured by daily active users, repeat visits, and farmer usefulness.

#### Pillar 2: Market Access

The Marketplace exists to help agriculture move.

It connects farmers, suppliers, buyers, and services.

The Marketplace should never feel like a classified website. It should feel curated, trusted, and easy to navigate.

Core categories:

- Fresh Produce
- Farm Inputs
- Farm Services
- Livestock
- Logistics and Transport
- Packaging and Storage

Future categories:

- Finance
- Insurance
- Equipment
- Cold Chain

#### Pillar 3: Trust

Trust is the foundation of Ghana Growers.

Without trust there is no marketplace. Without trust there is no network.

Trust is built through:

- Verification
- GG Quality Standard
- Professional profiles
- Clear communication
- Reliable sourcing

Every product should reinforce trust. Never weaken it.

#### Pillar 4: Connections

Ghana Growers exists to create meaningful connections, not just listings.

Connections include:

- Buyer to farmer
- Buyer to supplier
- Farmer to supplier
- Farmer to service provider
- Partner to farmer

Every interaction should lead people closer to opportunity.

#### Pillar 5: Growth

Everything should help users grow.

Farmers grow businesses.

Suppliers grow customers.

Buyers grow supply networks.

Ghana Growers grows agriculture.

### The Product Pyramid

Level 1: Trust

- Verification
- GG Quality Standard
- Professional profiles
- Reliable communication

Level 2: Daily Value

- Farmer Hub
- Knowledge
- Market prices
- Weather
- Crop health

Level 3: Connections

- Marketplace
- Buyer requests
- Supplier directory
- Farmer directory
- Lead management

Level 4: Business

- Featured memberships
- Supplier promotion
- Advertising
- Enterprise buyers
- Partnerships
- Future payments

Trust supports everything.

Daily value creates habit.

Connections create opportunity.

Business becomes sustainable.

### Primary Products

Ghana Growers has four flagship products.

#### Farmer Hub

Purpose: Help farmers make better decisions every day.

Primary users: Farmers.

Secondary users: Students, extension officers, and agricultural enthusiasts.

Success metric: Daily usage.

#### Marketplace

Purpose: Enable trusted agricultural trade.

Primary users: Buyers, farmers, and suppliers.

Success metric: Successful connections.

#### Network

Purpose: Help users discover trusted farmers and suppliers.

Primary users: Buyers, partners, and investors.

Success metric: Profile views, connection requests, and verified members.

#### Ghana Growers Quality Standard

Purpose: Increase trust, reward professionalism, and create differentiation.

Success metric: Member adoption and buyer confidence.

### Product Relationships

Farmer Hub creates daily engagement.

Marketplace creates business opportunities.

The Network builds trust.

GG Quality Standard strengthens credibility.

Together they create one ecosystem.

No product competes with another.

Each product supports the next.

### Future Ecosystem

The platform should be designed so new products fit naturally.

Possible future additions:

- Logistics Network
- Finance
- Micro-loans
- Insurance
- Equipment Rental
- Agricultural Events
- Learning Academy
- Farm Jobs
- Mobile App
- Community Groups
- Export Marketplace

Every future product must strengthen an existing pillar. Never build disconnected products.

### What We Will Not Become

We will not become:

- A generic ecommerce website
- A social media platform
- A news website
- A directory with no quality control
- A platform that tries to solve every agricultural problem

Focus creates strength.

### Product Decision Framework

Before building any feature ask:

- Does it improve trust?
- Does it improve daily value?
- Does it improve market access?
- Does it improve connections?
- Does it improve growth?

If the answer is no to all five, do not build it.

### Success Definition

Ghana Growers succeeds when:

- Farmers visit every week because Farmer Hub helps them.
- Buyers trust the platform enough to source produce through it.
- Suppliers see Ghana Growers as their preferred route to reach farmers.
- Verified members become recognised for professionalism.
- Agriculture becomes easier because Ghana Growers exists.

### Final Principle

We are not building a marketplace with extra tools.

We are building an agricultural ecosystem where every product strengthens every other product.

That ecosystem, not any single feature, is Ghana Growers' competitive advantage.

They will remember:

## Chapter 5: Farmer Hub

Version 1.0

### Introduction

Farmer Hub is the daily value engine of Ghana Growers.

It is not just another website page.

It is the product that gives farmers a reason to return.

Marketplace creates transactions.

The Network creates visibility.

The Quality Standard creates trust.

Farmer Hub creates habit.

If Farmer Hub succeeds, Ghana Growers becomes part of the farmer's daily life.

### Why Farmer Hub Exists

Farmers do not wake up wanting to use software.

They wake up needing to make decisions.

- Should I spray today?
- What is wrong with my crop?
- What are prices doing?
- Where can I find advice?
- How can I find buyers?

Farmer Hub exists to help farmers make better decisions every day.

It should feel like a daily farming companion.

Not a dashboard.

Not a software tool.

Not a page full of widgets.

### Product Position

Farmer Hub is Ghana Growers' flagship free product.

It should be easy to access.

It should appear early in the navigation.

It should be promoted clearly on the homepage.

It should not be hidden behind registration.

Farmers should be able to experience value before being asked to join.

### Core Purpose

Farmer Hub helps farmers with four daily needs:

1. Understand today's farming conditions.
2. Diagnose crop problems.
3. Ask practical farming questions.
4. Check market information before selling.

Everything inside Farmer Hub should support one of these needs.

If a feature does not help farmers make better farming decisions, it does not belong inside Farmer Hub.

### Core Tools

#### Crop Health

Crop Health is the strongest and most distinctive tool inside Farmer Hub.

It allows farmers to upload a crop photo and receive practical guidance.

It should be:

- Simple
- Visual
- Fast
- Actionable

The upload area should not feel empty or oversized.

The result should feel premium and concise.

Preferred result structure:

- Diagnosis
- Confidence
- Recommended Action
- Watch For
- Severity
- Optional Learn More

The response should not feel like a long AI essay.

It should feel like a practical farming note.

#### Live Weather Updates

Weather supports daily decisions.

It should help farmers decide:

- Can I spray today?
- Should I harvest today?
- Should I protect produce from rain?
- Should I delay field work?

Weather should not dominate Farmer Hub.

Weather is supporting information, not the main product.

Preferred structure:

- Temperature
- Rain Chance
- Humidity
- Wind
- Today's Recommendation
- View 7-Day Forecast

Avoid technical weather language.

Avoid API explanations.

Focus on farming decisions.

#### Farm Assistant

Farm Assistant helps farmers ask practical questions.

It should not answer like a general chatbot.

It should answer like a calm agricultural advisor.

Responses must be:

- Short
- Practical
- Simple
- Action-oriented

Preferred answer format:

- One short summary
- Three to five bullet points
- One Recommended Action
- One Warning only if necessary

Avoid:

- Long essays
- Overconfident claims
- Technical jargon
- Unnecessary explanations

If the question is medical, legal, financial, dangerous, or uncertain, the assistant should clearly advise the user to consult a qualified professional or agricultural extension officer.

#### Current Market Prices

Market Prices help farmers and buyers understand value before negotiation.

They should not feel like a spreadsheet first.

They should feel like market intelligence.

Preferred structure:

- Today's Market Snapshot
- Top Gainers
- Top Losers
- Popular Crops
- View Detailed Market Prices

The detailed table is useful, but it should not be the first thing users see.

### Farmer Hub Navigation

Farmer Hub should be task-based.

Navigation should focus on what farmers need to do.

Preferred tool order:

1. Crop Health
2. Live Weather Updates
3. Farm Assistant
4. Current Market Prices

No unnecessary tools.

Learning content belongs under Learn.

Community content belongs elsewhere.

Farmer Hub should remain focused on daily farming decisions.

### Today's Snapshot

Farmer Hub may include a compact daily snapshot.

This should be small and useful.

It should not repeat the full weather tool.

It may show:

- Location
- Temperature
- Rain Chance
- Today's Recommendation

If the snapshot becomes repetitive or cluttered, reduce it.

Farmer Hub should never feel like the same information repeated in different places.

### Daily Insights

Farmer Hub should feel alive.

It should not feel static.

Daily Insights may include:

- Weather Tip
- Market Watch
- Crop Alert
- Selling Opportunity
- Seasonal Reminder
- Regional Notice

These should be short.

Maximum one or two lines.

The goal is not to overwhelm farmers.

The goal is to give them one useful thing to notice.

### Mobile Experience

Farmer Hub must be designed mobile first.

Most farmers will access it on mobile.

Rules:

- No horizontal scrolling
- Large tap areas
- Simple stacked cards
- Short text
- Clear tool selection
- Upload actions should be easy
- Results should be readable on small screens

If something only works well on desktop, it is not finished.

### Language and Writing

Farmer Hub should use simple farming language.

Preferred:

- Upload crop photo
- Check today's weather
- Ask a farming question
- See market prices

Avoid:

- AI-powered diagnostics
- Advanced agricultural intelligence
- Comprehensive data platform

Farmers should understand the tool within seconds.

### AI Philosophy

AI inside Farmer Hub must be helpful, humble, and safe.

It should never pretend to be certain when it is not.

It should never give dangerous recommendations.

It should not replace agricultural extension officers.

It should support farmers with practical guidance and encourage expert help when needed.

AI should feel like a helpful assistant.

Not a professor.

Not a salesman.

Not a doctor.

### Trust and Safety

Farmer Hub should make clear that advice is guidance, not a guarantee.

Crop Health results should include uncertainty where appropriate.

Weather data should be treated as advisory.

Market prices should be indicative.

Farmers should not be misled into thinking every answer is final.

Trust grows when the platform is honest about limits.

### What Does Not Belong in Farmer Hub

Farmer Hub should not become crowded.

The following should not be added unless there is a clear reason:

- Long articles
- General news
- Community discussions
- Random promotions
- Complex dashboards
- Large advertisements
- Unrelated services

Everything should support daily farming decisions.

### Relationship to the Marketplace

Farmer Hub should naturally lead to the Marketplace.

Examples:

After checking crop health:

Need inputs?

Find suppliers.

After checking prices:

Ready to sell?

Explore Marketplace.

After asking about buyers:

Submit produce listing.

These suggestions should be subtle.

Farmer Hub should not feel like an advertisement.

Its first job is to help.

Business comes after usefulness.

### Relationship to Learn

Learn is for deeper education.

Farmer Hub is for quick decisions.

Example:

Farmer Hub says:

Tomato blight risk may increase this week.

Learn explains:

How to identify and manage tomato blight.

Farmer Hub should guide users to Learn when more detail is needed.

### Success Metrics

Farmer Hub success should be measured by:

- Repeat visits
- Daily active users
- Crop photo uploads
- Questions asked
- Market price views
- Weather checks
- Clicks from Farmer Hub to Marketplace
- Return rate
- Farmer satisfaction

The most important question:

Do farmers return because Farmer Hub helps them?

### Future Vision

Farmer Hub should eventually become personalized.

Future possibilities:

- Regional weather
- Crop-specific advice
- Saved crops
- Saved location
- Personal farm profile
- Disease alerts
- Buyer opportunities
- Supplier suggestions
- Seasonal reminders
- Voice input
- Local language support
- Mobile app notifications
- Offline-friendly features

The future Farmer Hub should feel like:

Good morning.

Here is what matters for your farm today.

### Final Principle

Farmer Hub is not a feature.

It is the daily farming companion of Ghana Growers.

It should give value before asking for anything.

It should help first.

It should build trust through usefulness.

If farmers return to Farmer Hub every week, the entire Ghana Growers ecosystem becomes stronger.

### Introduction

The Ghana Growers Marketplace is not an online shop.

It is a trusted agricultural marketplace where farmers, buyers, and suppliers discover opportunities and build long-term business relationships.

The Marketplace should never feel crowded.

It should never feel like a classified ads website.

It should feel curated.

Professional.

Reliable.

Simple.

The Marketplace exists to make buying and selling agricultural products easier.

### Why The Marketplace Exists

Farmers need visibility.

Buyers need reliable supply.

Suppliers need access to customers.

The Marketplace exists to connect these three groups through trust rather than volume.

The goal is not to have the most listings.

The goal is to have the most trusted listings.

### Marketplace Philosophy

The Marketplace should answer one question:

Can I confidently find what I need?

Everything else is secondary.

The Marketplace is built around:

- Trust
- Discovery
- Quality
- Connections

Not quantity.
