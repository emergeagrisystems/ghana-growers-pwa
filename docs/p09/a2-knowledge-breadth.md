## Safety and known gaps

No feed inclusion percentage/formulation, veterinary treatment, complete-feed assurance, guaranteed animal growth, unrestricted waste feedstock, contamination clearance, commercial approval, exact frass rate or guaranteed return. High-risk requests use the existing zero-credit refusal path. Unsafe generated output is rejected through the existing failed-answer recovery path. Negative tests include disguised approval claims such as “No permits required.”

Outstanding knowledge gaps: full GS 1382 requirements and current product-specific regulatory procedure; approved substrate/product-specific safety controls; validated processing and storage procedures; animal/stage-specific nutrition; assessed frass use; verified live course dates, starter-stock suppliers, customer demand and local costs. The pack states these limits instead of filling them from model memory. Exact training availability must be confirmed by the farmer with the named institution.

Mama G is not professionally certified. External agronomic/post-harvest certification remains **DEFERRED — PLANNED POST-LAUNCH**, not an initial-launch blocker or an automatic next gate. New guidance remains subject to source/provenance checks, automated evaluation, Protected Preview checks, uncertainty boundaries, founder review and later user testing. Precise high-risk operational claims remain restricted regardless of the certification schedule.
